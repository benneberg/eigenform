# Eigenform: Purpose & Intent

## Target Audience
Eigenform is designed for professionals and organizations managing complex, adaptive systems where traditional observability fails to provide context-aware insights.

### Key Demographics
- **System Architects**: Designing resilient, scalable topologies.
- **SRE (Site Reliability Engineers)**: Managing stability under high entropy.
- **Platform Engineers**: Building specialized observability internal tools.
- **Cyberneticists & System Theorists**: Exploring the relationship between observers and systems.

### Interests
- **Dynamic Observability**: Moving beyond static dashboards.
- **Adaptive UI**: Interfaces that morph based on user intent.
- **Cognitive Load Reduction**: Filtering noise to find meaningful signal.
- **Predictive Diagnostics**: Identifying "unknown unknowns".

### Pain Points
- **Dashboard Fatigue**: Being overwhelmed by thousands of non-contextual metrics.
- **The "Unknown Unknown" Problem**: Missing critical failures because they weren't predefined as metrics.
- **Fixed Perspectives**: Observability tools that treat every user (SRE, Security, Biz) the same.

## Value Proposition
Eigenform transforms raw system data into **meaningful narratives** by collapsing system states relative to the observer's specific intent.

### Unique Benefits
- **Intent-Driven Filtering (Satisfice)**: Only see what matters to your specific role and goal.
- **Pure Observation (Teller)**: Get raw, non-judgmental facts about system transitions, paired with context-sensitive interpretation.
- **Gap Discovery (Aporia)**: Proactively identify unmeasured complexity and blind spots in your telemetry.
- **Latent Topology**: Understand the potential of your system, not just the active state.

### Competitive Differentiation
Unlike standard observability platforms (Datadog, Grafana) that focus on *accumulating* data, Eigenform focuses on the *relationship* between the data and the observer. It treats meaning as an emergent property of observation, not a static attribute of the system.

## Core Features
- **Satisfice Engine**: A minimal, stable substrate definition that serves as the foundation for intent-based rendering.
- **Teller Narration**: A real-time engine that translates substrate events into human-readable, non-judgmental logs.
- **Aporia Probing**: A diagnostic tool for identifying gaps between substrate reality and observer measurement.
- **Intent Inference**: Dynamic mapping of observer roles (SRE, Security, etc.) to specific system collapse rules.
- **Diagnostic Stream**: A real-time ticker of system events and "collapse" history.
